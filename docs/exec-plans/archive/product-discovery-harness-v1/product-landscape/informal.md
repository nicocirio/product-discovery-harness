# Informal source — Product landscape

Give a product owner a reliable answer to “how are we with product ideas?” by
generating a concise, repository-local landscape table. It must show each real
opportunity or feature, a link only when its durable detail file exists, a
human-readable lifecycle/next action, and how long since meaningful review.
Old records must be surfaced for human review, never automatically discarded.
The feature must not depend on Engineering Harness installation or execution.
