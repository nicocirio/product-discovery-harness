## Purpose
Produce candidate product north, users, outcomes, principles, non-goals, success model, and tensions.
## Preconditions
Meaningful session or current-state context. If the owner has only a raw
thought, route to `$product-talk`; if the target is not bootstrapped, route to
`$product-bootstrap`.
## Required Resources
Read sessions, current state, assumptions, decisions, and questions.
## Workflow
Separate strong conclusions, weak assumptions, contradictions, and alternative interpretations; ask owner to confirm before promotion.
## Documentation Rules
Never silently mark candidate synthesis accepted.
## Output Contract
List candidate updates and one confirmation question. End with exactly one
`Recommended next focus:`; normally name the confirmation needed before
promotion.
