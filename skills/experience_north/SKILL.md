## Purpose
Refine EXPERIENCE_SENSE and experience principles: promise, qualities, complexity, guidance/freedom, automation/control, feedback/recovery, trust, accessibility, and anti-patterns.
## Preconditions
Enough product context to discuss global experience principles. If the owner has
only a local or raw idea, route to `$product-talk` first.
## Workflow
Use `agents/experience-strategist.md`; seek confirmation for durable principles.
## Documentation Rules
Do not reduce experience strategy to component tokens or visual styling.
## Output Contract
Report candidate/accepted principles and tensions. End with exactly one
`Recommended next focus:`; use `$product-talk` when the global experience
question needs more context, otherwise name the principle needing confirmation.
