# Vendor-neutral design brief

Generate four materially different interaction models for the same user outcome. They must differ in mental model, navigation, hierarchy, decision sequence, and degree of automation. Do not produce visual skins of the same solution.

Cover primary, first-use, repeat-use, empty, loading, error, ambiguity, permission, high-volume, and responsive states as applicable. Explain how each model improves outcome fit, learnability, trust, accessibility, and repeat-use efficiency.
